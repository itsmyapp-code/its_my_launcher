### 2. Core Remit & Responsibilities

The Data Privacy Lead assumes operational ownership over the following information governance pillars:

1. **Consent Infrastructure Enforcement:**  
   Ensuring all active app templates strictly deploy the "Equal Prominence" cookie layouts, execute first-layer opt-outs, and block marketing tracking scripts by default until positive opt-in is registered.

2. **Data Subject Rights Execution:**  
   Managing, verifying, and fulfilling user Subject Access Requests (SARs) or "Right to Erasure" (deletion) commands within the statutory one-month window, using "reasonable and proportionate" search criteria under DUAA parameters.

3. **Security & Encryption Auditing:**  
   Maintaining architectural overwatch to ensure production databases, storage handling, and API syncs utilize end-to-end TLS/SSL transmission and role-based validation.

4. **Incident & Breach Governance:**  
   Maintaining readiness to isolate system vulnerabilities and, if a high-risk data breach occurs, execute formal notification procedures to the Information Commission within the strict 72-hour window.
